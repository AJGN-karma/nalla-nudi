## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students from **Kannada-medium backgrounds** face difficulty understanding **technical English vocabulary**, even when they understand the concepts.

This results in:

* Reduced academic performance
* Lack of confidence in exams & interviews
* Difficulty transitioning to higher education

---

## 💡 Solution

**Nalla-Nudi** provides a structured learning platform:

* 📖 English → Kannada technical translation
* 🧠 Simple contextual explanations
* 🔊 Pronunciation support
* ⭐ Personalized revision tools
